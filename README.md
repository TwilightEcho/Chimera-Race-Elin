This adds **two** new races and jobs:

Races: Chimera, Kitsune

Jobs: Apex Predator (Chimera), Celestial Traveler (Kitsune)

Neither races are balanced, they were made by me for roleplaying, Chimera is based around the RP concept of being a failed experiment by a mad alchemist to become a synthetic god; the experiment failed, but the body survived; it was built from an amalgamation of things, and fueled with an Arcane Core, hence the heavy magic focus and low HP. 

Kitsune is similar, still very overpowered, 1 hp race with excessive magical power, based on Kitsune Mythology (Moonlit Kin, etc.) and heavy magic focus.

The unique jobs just finish off their kit.

KitsuneStaringSpell adds Spear of the Moonlight (with 0 charges, since I use unlimited spells) to the Celeestial Traveler job (for Kitsune, it goes inside the BepinEX/Plugins folder.)
